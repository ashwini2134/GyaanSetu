# Testing

Test each difficulty level:

1. **Easy:** Guess numbers until you win — verify range is 1-50 and you get 10 guesses.
2. **Medium:** Guess numbers — verify range is 1-100 and you get 7 guesses.
3. **Hard:** Guess numbers — verify range is 1-200 and you get 5 guesses.
4. Test running out of guesses — verify the correct number is revealed.
5. Test entering an invalid difficulty choice.
