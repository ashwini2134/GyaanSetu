# Personalized Story Generator

Write a Python program that asks the user for their name, age, favorite color, and hobby, then prints a short personalized story using their answers.

**Example interaction:**
```
Enter your name: Maya
Enter your age: 22
Enter your favorite color: blue
Enter your hobby: painting

--- Your Story ---
Once upon a time, there was a person named Maya.
At 22 years old, Maya loved the color blue more than anything.
Every day after work, Maya would spend hours painting.
And that is how Maya lived a colorful and happy life!
```

The story should use f-strings to embed the user's input into the narrative. The exact story wording is up to you — be creative!
